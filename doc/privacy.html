Privacy Policy for My Voice Buttons

Last Updated: October 18, 2025

Summary: My Voice Buttons is a completely offline app that does not collect, store, or transmit any user data whatsoever.

Overview

My Voice Buttons is a simple assistive communication app designed to help users communicate through audio buttons. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Specifically:

  • We do not collect any user information
  • We do not track usage or analytics
  • We do not store any data on our servers
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services
  • We do not require user accounts or registration

Data Storage

All app functionality operates entirely on your device:

  • Audio files are bundled with the app and stored locally
  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • No user-generated content is saved

Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions beyond what is required to play audio.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party services.

Children's Privacy

This app is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
